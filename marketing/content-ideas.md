# Notabl — Content Marketing Ideas (Dental Practice Owners)

20 content ideas, grouped by the angle they support. Each is written so it
could become a blog post, LinkedIn post, or short email — the "template"
section at the bottom shows the reusable structure so future pieces can be
generated (by a person or an AI assist) without starting from scratch each
time.

## Reusable template for future pieces

```
TITLE: [specific, concrete claim or question — avoid vague titles]

HOOK (1-2 sentences):
State the tension/problem this piece addresses. Ground it in something
true and general about dental practices, not a fabricated statistic.

BODY (3-5 short sections):
- Each section makes one clear point
- Use only real, sourced data OR clearly-labeled illustrative examples
  (never present synthetic/demo numbers as real without a "(illustrative
  example)" or "(demo data)" label)
- Keep sentences short; this audience is busy and not marketing-fluent

TAKEAWAY (1 sentence):
What the reader should do or think differently after reading.

CTA (optional, 1 line):
Soft — "See a sample Notabl report" rather than a hard sell.
```

## Sourcing rule

Any piece that cites a statistic ("X% of practices…") must link to or name
a real source, or be explicitly reframed as an illustrative example using
Notabl's own demo dataset. Never present a made-up number as fact —
this applies to marketing content exactly as strictly as it applies to the
product itself (see docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §15, Legal/Safety Rules).
