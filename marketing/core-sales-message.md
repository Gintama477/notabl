# Notabl — Core Sales Message

## The message, in one sentence

Notabl reads every patient review so you don't have to, and tells you
what's changing before it becomes a real problem.

## What to lead with (in priority order)

1. **Time saved.** You already have the information you need (your
   reviews) — you just don't have time to read all of them, cross-reference
   them, and notice patterns. Notabl does that in the background.
2. **Catching complaints early.** A single bad review is a data point. Four
   reviews mentioning the same thing in three weeks is a pattern. Most
   practice owners only see the individual data points — Notabl
   shows the pattern.
3. **Improving the patient experience.** Not as an abstract mission
   statement — as a direct consequence of #2. Fix the pattern, the reviews
   (and referrals) improve.
4. **Trends over individual reviews.** The value isn't "read this one
   review" — you can already do that. The value is "here's what's
   increasing, here's what's new, here's what's actually worth your
   attention this week out of everything that came in."

## What NOT to lead with

- **"AI."** It's the mechanism, not the benefit. Practice owners don't buy
  AI; they buy "know what's going wrong before it gets worse." If AI comes
  up, it's in service of explaining how the report is possible, not why
  they should want it.
- **Vanity metrics.** "Sentiment score" and "NPS-style dashboards" sound
  like something built for a marketing team, not a solo practice owner
  between patients. Speak in plain language: "more patients mentioned wait
  times this week."
- **Fear-based messaging about reputation damage.** True, but overused in
  this space and reads as generic scare-copy. Calm and useful beats
  alarming.

## One-line elevator pitches (situational)

- **Cold outreach subject line:** "A pattern in your recent reviews"
- **Landing page hero:** "Know what your patients are saying before small
  problems become big ones."
- **In-person / phone:** "You're already getting the reviews. We just make
  sure you actually see what they add up to, every week, without reading
  them all yourself."
- **Pricing objection response:** "It's less than the cost of one missed
  appointment slot a month, and it's designed to help you catch the kind of
  small operational issues — like scheduling delays — that cause missed
  appointments in the first place."
