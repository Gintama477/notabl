# Notabl — Landing Page Hero Copy Variants

Five different hero-message concepts to test. Variant A is the one
currently live on the site (app/page.tsx). Swapping in another variant is a
copy change only — no layout/design change needed.

---

## Variant A — "Before it becomes a problem" (current default)

**Headline:** Know what your patients are saying before small problems
become big ones.

**Subheadline:** Notabl analyzes your patient reviews and sends you a
clear weekly report showing praise, complaints, emerging issues, and what
deserves your attention.

**Angle:** Prevention / early warning. Best for owners who are anxious
about reputation but haven't had a specific bad incident recently.

---

## Variant B — "Stop reading reviews one at a time"

**Headline:** Stop reading patient reviews one at a time.

**Subheadline:** Notabl reads all of them for you and tells you what's
actually changing — so you spend five minutes a week instead of an hour.

**Angle:** Time savings, very literal. Best for owners who've explicitly
complained about the volume of reviews or time spent on admin tasks.

---

## Variant C — "What's really going on at your practice"

**Headline:** What are your patients really saying about your practice?

**Subheadline:** Individual reviews only tell part of the story.
Notabl finds the patterns — what's improving, what's not, and what's
new — so you always know where you stand.

**Angle:** Curiosity / clarity. Best for owners who check reviews
occasionally but don't feel like they have the full picture.

---

## Variant D — "Built for practices, not marketers"

**Headline:** A weekly review report built for dentists, not marketing
teams.

**Subheadline:** No dashboards to learn, no jargon. Just a clear weekly
summary of what your patients loved, what they didn't, and what to do about
it.

**Angle:** Simplicity / anti-complexity. Best for owners skeptical of
"software" generally, who want reassurance this isn't going to be a time
sink to learn.

---

## Variant E — "Catch it before the next review"

**Headline:** The next bad review is preventable. Here's how to see it
coming.

**Subheadline:** Notabl tracks which complaints are increasing in your
patient reviews — appointment delays, phone responsiveness, billing
confusion — so you can fix the pattern before more patients hit it.

**Angle:** Direct, slightly more urgent. Best for performance-marketing
channels (paid ads) where a sharper hook earns the click; softer on organic
/ referral traffic where Variant A or C reads better.

---

## Testing notes

- Run one variant at a time for at least 2 weeks or 100 visitors, whichever
  is longer, before switching (see marketing/weekly-marketing-report-design.md
  for how to track this).
- Keep the secondary CTA ("See Sample Report") identical across all
  variants — it's the highest-intent, lowest-friction next step and
  shouldn't be a variable in the test.
- Do not test more than one variant live at the same time without an actual
  A/B testing setup (out of scope for MVP) — swap sequentially and compare
  period over period instead.
