# Validation Tracker — How to Use It

`tracker.csv` is a simple spreadsheet, not a CRM — open it in Excel,
Google Sheets, or Numbers and fill it in as you go. One row per practice
you contact.

## Columns

- **business_name** — the practice's name
- **contact_name** — who you emailed/messaged (owner, office manager, etc.)
- **email** — their email address
- **date_contacted** — when you sent the initial outreach (YYYY-MM-DD)
- **response** — did they reply? (e.g. "yes", "no", "out of office")
- **interested** — yes / no / maybe, based on their reply
- **pilot_started** — yes / no — did you grant them pilot access (see the
  Pilot Access section of `/admin`)?
- **feedback_received** — yes / no — did they submit the 8-question
  feedback form, or give you feedback some other way?
- **willing_to_pay** — yes / no / unsure, based on what they told you
- **converted_to_customer** — yes / no — did they become a real paying
  customer?
- **notes** — anything else worth remembering about the conversation

## Why this is intentionally simple

This is meant to answer one question — "where does each practice stand?" —
not to manage a sales pipeline with stages, reminders, or automation. If
outreach grows well past 50 practices and this genuinely becomes limiting,
that's the point to consider a real CRM — not before.
