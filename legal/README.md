# Legal Placeholder Documents

The actual content for these placeholder legal documents lives directly in
the app so there's a single source of truth (no risk of the rendered page
and a separate markdown source drifting out of sync):

- Terms of Service: `app/legal/terms/page.tsx`
- Privacy Policy: `app/legal/privacy/page.tsx`
- AI Disclaimer: `app/legal/ai-disclaimer/page.tsx`

All three are explicitly labeled in-app as placeholders that have not been
reviewed by an attorney (see `components/marketing/LegalPageShell.tsx`).
Have these reviewed and finalized by a qualified lawyer before relying on
them for a real, operating business — this applies especially before
collecting real customer/patient-adjacent data or charging real customers.
